Biography

I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science at Istanbul Technical University (expected 2026), an admitted M.S. student at New York University, and a B.Sc. graduate from the University of the People, Pasadena, CA. My research focuses on reliable and ethical AI systems that translate into scalable deployment, spanning applied machine learning and data-driven systems in both academic and industry settings—including BRAC Research and ASELSAN.


My work has been recognized with the Mercedes-Benz Fellowship (2025) and a YTB Fully Funded Scholarship (2023). I currently serve as a peer reviewer for ICML 2026 and have received Distinguished Reviewer recognition from the IEEE Internet of Things Journal.
